This notebook keeps your team's notes **map by map**: protocols, defensive setups, executes, retakes, lineups, anti-strats. The **General** map holds what applies everywhere (comms, economy, timeouts).

## Getting started

- **New note** (top right): a title, a map, a type. Each type starts from a **template** (roles, timings, utility…) that you adapt.
- **Pin** your key protocols with the **Pinned** toggle: they move to the top of their map and into the **match sheet**, a condensed page to keep in front of you during a timeout.
- **Paste a screenshot** (`Ctrl+V`) into the editor: the image is stored in the notebook and inserted into the note. Perfect for a lineup.

## Copy and paste

- **Copy** a note, a map or a match sheet, then paste it into Discord (Markdown, aligned tables) or into Word, Google Docs, Notion (formatting kept). The `[ v ]` button next to it also offers plain text and the full Markdown file.
- **Paste a protocol**: `Ctrl+V` on a notebook page creates a note from the copied text. Word or Docs formatting is converted, and the first heading becomes the note's title.
- **Import .md files** (Obsidian, another notebook): drop them onto a map.

## Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `/` | search (filters: `map:ascent type:retake side:def #tag`) |
| `N` | new note |
| `E` | edit the note or map on screen |
| `F` | the map's match sheet |
| `C` | copy the note, map or sheet on screen |
| `[` `]` | previous / next map |
| `Ctrl+Enter` | finish editing (saving is automatic) |
| `Esc` | go back |

## Writing

The editor toolbar applies formatting for you, and **Insert** offers ready-made blocks (roles table, timings, checklist, callout). To write by hand:

- Headings: `## Roles`
- Lists: `- heaven smoke`, checkboxes: `- [ ] veto`
- Tables: `| Player | Agent |`
- Link to another note: `[[retake-b]]`, or to another map: `[[bind/setup-a]]`
- Callout: `> [!WARNING]` on one line, then the text below it with `>`

## Your notebook
